Mandatory Gulf Recruiting Requirements

🏥

DHA / MOH / HAAD Licensing

For nurses and healthcare professionals, state your DHA Eligibility Letter status, MOH evaluation, or HAAD/DOH license number clearly at the top of your resume.

🛎️

Hospitality PMS & Star Rating

For hotel, F&B, and resort roles, list the star rating of past properties (e.g. 5-Star Marriott / Hilton), Opera PMS / Micros POS fluency, and guest rating KPIs.

🇦🇪

Visa Status & Immediate Joining

Candidates already inside the UAE on a Visit Visa or with a cancelled Employment Visa who can join immediately get 4x more interview calls from Gulf agencies.

Personal & Gulf Identification Header

Professional Photo, Nationality, Marital Status, Current Visa Status (Visit / Employment / Golden), Passport Validity, and UAE Phone (+971) or WhatsApp number.

Clinical or Operational Scope

Frequently Asked Questions

What information is mandatory on a nursing CV for Dubai and the UAE?

Dubai healthcare recruiters require: DHA / MOH / DOH license eligibility or registration number, BLS/ACLS certifications, total bed capacity of current hospital, department/ward specializations (ICU, OT, ER, NICU), visa status, nationality, and passport validity.

What do 5-star hotel recruiters in Dubai look for in a hospitality CV?

UAE hospitality recruiters look for property ratings (e.g. 5-Star Luxury, Boutique), Property Management Systems used (Opera PMS, Micros, Fidelio), guest satisfaction metrics, multilingual proficiencies, and current notice period or ready-to-join availability.

Should a Gulf CV include a photo and personal details?

Yes. In the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and GCC countries, including a professional corporate headshot, nationality, marital status, and visa status is standard and expected by recruitment agencies and hospital HR departments.
