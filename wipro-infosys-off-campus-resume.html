Frequently Asked Questions

What resume format is preferred by Infosys and Wipro recruiters?

Infosys and Wipro talent acquisition teams prefer a clean, 1-page reverse-chronological format without graphical rating bars, icons, or text boxes. Education, Technical Projects, and Core Coding Skills must be readily scanable.

What technical skills should be highlighted for Infosys DSE and Specialist Programmer roles?

For higher-tier packages like Infosys DSE or Specialist Programmer, emphasize Data Structures & Algorithms, Java/Spring Boot, Python, Cloud computing (AWS/Azure), React/Node.js, and competitive programming credentials.

Can freshers with career gaps apply for Wipro and Infosys?

Yes, both companies allow up to 1-2 years of academic gap between 10th, 12th, and graduation, provided there are no active backlogs at the time of onboarding. Gaps should be clearly documented.
