Frequently Asked Questions

What technical skills should be featured on a Mechanical Engineering ATS resume?

Core skills to include are: CAD/CAM Software (AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CATIA, Creo, ANSYS), Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T), Manufacturing Processes (CNC/VMC, Welding, Casting, Sheet Metal), Quality Standards (ISO 9001, Six Sigma, 5S, Kaizen), and Root Cause Analysis (8D, Ishikawa).

How should mechanical freshers showcase final year academic projects?

Clearly define: (1) Project Title, (2) Software/Design tools used (e.g. SolidWorks & ANSYS FEA), (3) Key engineering calculations or simulations conducted, and (4) Measurable outcome (e.g. 'Reduced prototype weight by 14% while retaining structural safety factor 1.8').

Is single-column format mandatory for engineering resumes?

Yes. Engineering CVs frequently get parsed by Taleo, Workday, and SAP SuccessFactors. Single-column layouts ensure CAD software names, formulas, and technical abbreviations are read accurately by ATS parsers.
