Frequently Asked Questions

What ATS keywords must be included in an Accountant resume in India?

Critical accounting ATS keywords include: Tally Prime, Tally ERP 9, GST Filing (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9), TDS / TCS calculation, Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS), Accounts Payable (AP), Accounts Receivable (AR), Advanced Excel (VLOOKUP, Pivot Tables), and Balance Sheet finalization.

Can B.Com freshers build an ATS resume without prior experience?

Yes. Fresh commerce graduates should highlight academic coursework, Tally/GST certification projects, internship bookkeeping tasks, and ledger management to achieve a high ATS score.

How should accounting accomplishments be formatted on a CV?

Always quantify results: e.g., 'Reconciled ₹2.5 Cr monthly bank transactions with zero variance', 'Reduced vendor billing cycle by 18%', or 'Managed statutory GST filings for 40+ corporate clients on time'.
