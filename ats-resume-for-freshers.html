Frequently Asked Questions for Freshers

What is the best ATS resume format for freshers in India?

A clean, single-column reverse-chronological format where Education, Projects, and Core Competencies are front and center. Avoid icons, graphic skill bars, and multi-column tables which confuse ATS parsers.

How do I pass campus placement ATS filters for TCS, Infosys, and Wipro?

Ensure your resume contains exact skill keywords (e.g. Java, Python, SQL, Data Structures), quantifies college project outcomes with numbers, and includes your degree, board/university name, and passing year clearly.
