Frequently Asked Questions

Why do USA and UK resumes prohibit profile photos?

In the United States and the United Kingdom, strict Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) and anti-discrimination laws make recruiters instantly reject resumes containing photos, date of birth, or marital status to prevent unconscious bias lawsuits. Our builder automatically enforces compliance.

Why does Dubai (UAE) require visa status and photos?

In the UAE and Gulf region, companies must sponsor employment visas and manage travel logistics. Employers actively look for a professional headshot, current visa status (Visit/Employment/Golden Visa), nationality, and a valid UAE driving license upfront.
